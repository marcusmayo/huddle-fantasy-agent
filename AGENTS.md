# Video publication requirements

For current and future videos, Yahoo and Blitzkrieg are the only permitted league/account/fantasy-team identifiers. Remove other league names, opponent fantasy-team names and personal identifiers from footage, narration, captions, titles, previews, metadata and descriptions. Keep relevant Huddle/ChatGPT product attribution and NFL player/team information. Preserve original evidence privately; disclose editorial masks. Recording remains separate from the Huddle app. See docs/media/real-draft-previews.md.
